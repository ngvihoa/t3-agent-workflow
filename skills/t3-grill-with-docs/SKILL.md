# Clarify with docs

Read [policy](../t3-agent-workflow/references/policy.md). Load [grilling](../t3-grilling/SKILL.md) and [domain modeling](../t3-domain-modeling/SKILL.md). Ask unresolved decisions in rounds, grounded in project facts. Research facts yourself; use delegates only under the saved policy/runtime rules. Reuse previous answers.

Preserve existing glossary/ADR conventions. Capture resolved terms and real trade-offs, not every conversation detail. Confirm the intended outcome and material uncertainties. When a shared understanding exists, route to [to spec](../t3-to-spec/SKILL.md). Agreement to an interview summary is not approval of a spec that has not been presented yet.
